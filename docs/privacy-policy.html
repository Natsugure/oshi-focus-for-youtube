Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2025

Overview

Oshi Focus For YouTube ("the Extension") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data the Extension collects, uses, and stores.

Data Collection

The Extension stores the following data locally in your browser only:

1. User Settings

  • List of allowed YouTube channels (channel IDs and names)
  • Display/hide preferences
  • YouTube Shorts handling settings

This information is stored in your browser using Chrome Sync Storage API and may be synchronized with your Google Account.

Purpose of Data Usage

The collected data is used only for:

  • Displaying content only from channels you have allowed
  • Customizing YouTube display based on your settings
  • Maintaining your preferences across browser restarts

Data Storage Location

All data is stored locally in your browser only.

The Extension:

  • Does NOT send data to external servers
  • Does NOT share data with third parties
  • Does NOT use analytics or tracking tools
  • Works offline (except for YouTube itself)

Data Access

Only you can access your collected data. No third parties, including the developer, can access your data.

Data Deletion

You can delete your data by:

  1. Remove from settings: Delete channels from the allowed list individually
  2. Uninstall the extension: All data will be deleted
  3. Clear browser storage: Clear Chrome storage from chrome://settings/clearBrowserData

Permission Usage

The Extension uses the following permissions:

storage

Used to save your allowed channel list and settings.

tabs

Used to retrieve information about the currently active YouTube channel and enable the "Add Current Channel" feature.

host_permissions (youtube.com)

Used to operate only on YouTube, controlling sidebar and recommendation display, and blocking videos.

Third-Party Services

The Extension does not integrate with any third-party services. All processing is done locally.

Children's Privacy

The Extension does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to Privacy Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, we will post the changes on this page. Significant changes will be announced in the extension update notes.
